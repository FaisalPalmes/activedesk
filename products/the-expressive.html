The Expressive

For those who love to express themselves.

The Expressive is our entry-level desk for those creative people who love to get down and express their beautiful colourful self to the world. Honestly, I don't think you will be working from this desk, but more like busting moves, chatting, collaborating and expressing your unique gift into the world.

"Here's to the high fiving and busting moves at your desk — what back pain!"

Request a sample Download brochure

The desktop

25mm MFC laminate

The Expressive desktop is made from hardwearing laminate, so it can withstand any battering you may throw at it. It comes in 10 standard colourful colours — but don't let that stop you asking for more.

Standard decors

  • Cashmere

Features and benefits

  • Abrasion, impact and scratch-resistant
  • Moisture-resistant surface
  • Food-safe and hygienic
  • Lightfast
  • Durable and easy to maintain
  • Also available MED-certified
  • Antibacterial properties (tested to JIS Z 2801)
