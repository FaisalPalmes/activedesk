01 · Decisions I need

Three approvals unblock the build

Full reasoning is in STAGE0_PLAN.md in the repo. Everything else in the swap list can arrive during the build without blocking it.

Scope: build the pitch, not the whole site The spec asks for thirteen routes. That is a full commercial build, on your own time, for an October project. The homepage narrative, the finish selector and the legal footer are what win the engagement — the eleven inner pages are blocked on content from James anyway.

Drop the 3D desk — I am reversing my own recommendation The brand spec's rule is that a convincing CSS raise ships first. The desk below is that CSS raise, and it works. No library weight, no capability-gate risk, nothing to break on a cheap phone. Revisit it when real product photography exists — an image sequence from your own shoot would beat both options.

Accept the palette as a working placeholder Nothing here was sampled from their site — see the note below. The build needs some palette to proceed; this one is defensible and takes minutes to replace once SicoCreative's guidelines arrive.

02 · The signature mechanic

The logo stops being decoration and becomes the interface

Scroll drives one progress value. That value raises the desk from seated to standing and lifts the two underscores in the wordmark at the same time. Eased and weighted — a desk has mass, so it must never feel like a slider. When it completes, the underscores settle back into the idle loop running in the header now.

Below is the raise as it currently stands, in CSS. On mobile there is no pin and no scrub — a lighter reveal instead. With reduced motion preferred, the desk simply appears standing and all the copy is present.

Seated

The underscore is a system, not a logo quirk

It already runs into their SKUs — Active_01, Active_02, Active_03 — and into their bylines: By_ James C Scott. The rebuild extends it to section numbers, list markers, form-field underlines, hover states and loading indicators, so the brand reads as having a grammar rather than a mark.

One signature moment per page, and this is the homepage's. Everything else on the site is restraint.

06 · Working rules

The constraints this build holds to

Nothing invented

No prices — they publish none anywhere. No specs beyond the recovered set. No testimonials beyond the four verbatim ones. No new health claims. Every gap is a labelled placeholder tracked in the swap list, which doubles as the questionnaire for James.

The founder story, handled with care

It records a real bereavement. The emotional architecture — loss, investigation, mechanism, epiphany, mission, tribute — is preserved exactly, because the mechanism step is what makes it land rather than being mere sentiment. His personal photographs are never substituted. No design flourish trivialises it.

Motion discipline

  • One signature moment per page.
  • No scroll-jacking, ever.
  • Complete and handsome with motion disabled.
  • Mobile motion reduced aggressively, tested at 375px.
  • Every animation earns its place or it is cut.

Defects fixed, not reproduced

The live site's typos, truncated paragraphs, double-dot ellipses, mixed spellings and inconsistent brand casing are corrected in the rebuild. The full list is in the swap list. One of them is sensitive and is flagged there to be raised with James in person rather than in writing.
