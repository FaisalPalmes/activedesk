01 · Palette

Warm ground, deep ink, one confident accent

A gallery-warm bone ground rather than the cold white every desk brand defaults to — it flatters wood tones and reads premium immediately. One accent does all the conversion work, so every orange element on the page means something.

02 · Typography

Two families, clear division of labour

Display · Archivo

The Smooth Operator

A confident grotesque with genuine width range. Set tight and heavy it gives headlines the authority of a considered purchase, and it holds character at the enormous sizes the scroll narrative needs.

Body · Instrument Sans

Sitting for long unbroken periods is associated with health risks. Varying your posture through the day — sitting, standing, moving between the two — is the simplest change most desk workers can make.

Warm, highly legible, and not the default sans on every other site. Comfortable for the educational sections where the copy has to carry real reading weight.

03 · The signature motif

The mark rises. The desk rises.

Active Desk already writes itself as Act_ive De_sk. That underscore is the brand's own asset, and here it lifts — the mark performs the product's core action. Hover the wordmark in the header to see it. The same idea drives section markers, link states and, at full scale, the hero.

Below is a rough preview of the rise, built in CSS. In the real build this is a 3D desk, pinned in the viewport, with the telescoping legs driven directly by scroll position — weighted and physical rather than a simple slide.

Seated

Why this is the make-or-break section

Their current site has no motion and no product storytelling at all. A desk that visibly rises as you scroll is the single clearest demonstration of what a considered site does that a brochure page cannot — and it is the moment James will remember.

The shadow tightens and darkens as the desk stands, the copy fades through three stages beside it, and the whole sequence is reversible: scroll back up and the desk sits down again.

05 · Build decisions

How the 3D and the fallbacks work

A desk built from primitives

The desk is modelled in code rather than downloaded, for three reasons:

  • The legs must telescope in separately rigged segments — downloaded desk models are almost always a single fused mesh.
  • No licence risk of any kind.
  • Effectively no file to download, and the materials can be tied straight to the palette above.

The fallback ladder

  • Capable desktop: full 3D hero and scroll-driven rise.
  • Mobile: a small hero canvas only where it stays smooth, with the rise handled by a lightweight animated sequence.
  • No 3D support, low-end device, or reduced motion preferred: still images and simple fades.

The page is complete and persuasive without the 3D layer at all.

Facts discipline

Only verified facts appear as real: the four tier names and their materials, the health-led line, the dealer route, the brand stylisation. Prices, specs, warranty terms, delivery times and review quotes are visibly styled placeholders, every one of them tracked in a swap list for James to fill in.
